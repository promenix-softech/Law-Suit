Why change

The same suit — two ways of preparing it

A bank recovery suit is mostly repetition: the same facts typed into a notice, a plaint, an affidavit, a certificate and an index — then formatted for e-filing. That is where the hours go, and where errors creep in.

Features

Everything a bank-recovery practice needs

⇪Case intake

  • New case from a scan: facts read and filled in for checking
  • Bulk upload: one PDF with many loan files, split by account

  • Masters for banks, branches and courts

₹Calculations

  • Claim amount, interest and amount in words (lakh/crore)

  • Commercial-suit threshold and Sec 12A check

  • Demand notice — English and Marathi
  • Plaint: ordinary, Order XXXVII summary, commercial
  • List of documents, affidavit / statement of truth, vakalatnama

✓Approval and control

  • Drafter → approver workflow; approver ≠ drafter, enforced

  • Executed documents taken straight from the scan

  • Archive and restore cases
