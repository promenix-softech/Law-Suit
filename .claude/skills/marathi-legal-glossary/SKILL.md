## Rules
- Unicode only (no legacy fonts like Shree-Lipi/Kruti Dev). Font: Noto Serif Devanagari, 14 pt.
- Personal names: keep as in bank records; transliterate to Devanagari only when the bank record has it, else keep Roman script.
- Numbers: use Western digits (0–9) for amounts and dates unless the court requires Devanagari digits †.
- Amount in words: generate in code (Indian system: लाख, कोटी), not by LLM.
